01 — Summary

The short version

“Coffee is a repeatable craft — the job is making the good pour happen every single time, not just the first time.”

Mohannad has spent the last two years behind the bar at Culaccino Coffee House, working his way up to Head Barista through espresso calibration, manual brewing, and daily quality control. He holds SCA Barista Skills certification alongside additional training from Espresso Academy and Barista Hustle, covering everything from milk science and terroir to hospitality culture. Beyond his own cup, he trains other baristas — running latte art courses at Nader’s Coffee Hub and setting the standard new hires are held to at Culaccino.
